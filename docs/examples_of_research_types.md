Qualitative research; Systematically explores meanings, experiences, or social processes using non-numeric data; Semi-structured interviews; focus groups; ethnography.
Exploratory research; Open-ended inquiry used to clarify poorly understood problems and generate hypotheses; Scoping interviews with stakeholders; pilot surveys; exploratory factor analysis.
Basic research; Seeks fundamental understanding without an immediate practical goal; Mapping a signaling pathway; proving a mathematical property; studying memory formation in mice.
Mixed research; Integrates qualitative and quantitative methods in one program to gain complementary insights; Survey + interviews; RCT outcomes + focus groups; sequential explanatory design.
Experimental development; Uses existing knowledge systematically to produce or improve products, processes, or services; Prototype medical device; optimizing a drug formulation; refining a recommender system.
Theoretical research; Develops conceptual frameworks or formal models to explain phenomena, often without new data; Game-theoretic model; computational model of vision; cosmological theory paper.
Fundamental research; Advances general knowledge of basic principles with no immediate application required; Characterizing a new particle; describing a novel enzyme; axiomatizing a logic system.
Questionnaires; Structured instruments for collecting standardized responses; Likert patient satisfaction form; WHOQOL; usability checklist.
Applied research; Uses scientific methods to solve specific, practical problems in real contexts; Evaluating a triage protocol; reducing hospital wait times; improving churn prediction.
Quantitative research; Investigates phenomena using numerical data, measurement, and statistical analysis; RCTs; cohort studies with regression; weighted national surveys.
Experiment; A controlled study that manipulates an independent variable to test its causal effect; Randomized controlled trial; lab task with counterbalanced conditions; online A/B test.
Case study; In-depth analysis of a single case (or small set) within its real-world context; Rare disease patient; a hospital’s AI rollout; landmark court decision.
Longitudinal research; Follows the same units over time to track change and temporal relationships; 20-year birth cohort; quarterly employee panel; repeated rehab measures.
Action research; Collaborative cycles of planning, acting, observing, and reflecting to improve a local problem; Co-designing clinic handovers; iterative classroom interventions; community health campaign.
Secondary research; Synthesizes or analyzes existing data or literature rather than collecting new primary data; Systematic review; meta-analysis; reuse of national registry data.
Comparative research; Systematically compares two or more cases to identify similarities, differences, or patterns; Cross-country policy comparisons; multicenter care pathways; algorithm benchmarks.
Descriptive research; Characterizes the “what” of a population or phenomenon without testing causal hypotheses; Disease prevalence survey; customer segmentation; workload profiling.
Correlational research; Examines associations among variables without manipulation, not establishing causation; Sleep vs exam scores; BMI vs blood pressure; usage vs retention.
Explanatory research; Tests theories about why relationships occur, often focusing on mechanisms or mediators; Mediation analysis of stress→inflammation; path models; theory-driven RCTs.
Cross-sectional research; Measures a sample at a single point in time to estimate levels or associations; One-time national health survey; app user snapshot; point-prevalence audit.
Survey methodology; The science of designing, sampling, fielding, and analyzing surveys to produce valid estimates; Sampling frames; mode effects; questionnaire pretesting.
Causal research; Uses designs and analyses aimed at identifying causal effects under explicit assumptions; Randomized trials; natural experiments; difference-in-differences.
Field research; Collects data in natural (non-lab) settings; Participant observation in clinics; field experiments in markets; environmental sampling.
Deductive research; Starts from theory, derives testable hypotheses, and evaluates them with data; Testing a self-determination theory prediction; falsifying a neural model; preregistered tests.
Ethnography; Immersive observation of cultures or settings to understand norms and practices; Shadowing in wards; participant observation; field notes.
Grounded theory; Iteratively builds theory from data through constant comparison; Open/axial coding of interviews; memoing; theoretical sampling.
Phenomenology; Describes lived experience to reveal the essence of a phenomenon; In-depth interviews on chronic pain; reflective analysis; bracketing.
Narrative inquiry; Analyzes stories to understand how people make sense of events; Patient illness narratives; clinician career stories; life histories.
Content analysis; Systematically codes texts/media to identify patterns and themes; Coding social media posts; policy documents; news coverage.
Discourse analysis; Examines language-in-use to reveal framing, power, and meaning-making; Clinical notes rhetoric; policy speeches; doctor–patient talk.
Phenomenography; Maps the qualitatively different ways people experience or conceive something; Students’ conceptions of learning; clinicians’ views of AI.
Community-based participatory research (CBPR); Equitably partners with communities across all study phases to produce actionable knowledge; Community advisory boards; co-designed interventions; participatory evaluation.
Cohort study; Follows exposed and unexposed groups over time to estimate incidence and risk; Prospective vaccine cohort; retrospective occupational exposure cohort; registry-based cohort.
Case–control study; Compares prior exposures between cases and matched controls to estimate odds of association; Rare cancer risk factors; outbreak source tracing; pharmacovigilance signal.
Ecological study; Relates aggregate exposures to aggregate outcomes; County-level pollution vs mortality; country alcohol policy vs cirrhosis rates.
Diagnostic accuracy study; Estimates test performance against a reference standard; Sensitivity/specificity of a rapid test; ROC/AUC for imaging AI; likelihood ratios.
Interrupted time-series; Uses repeated measures to detect level/slope changes after an intervention; Policy rollout impact; stewardship program effects; UI change in apps.
Quasi-experimental (nonequivalent groups); Evaluates interventions without random assignment using design/analysis controls; Matched schools study; clinic before–after with control site; regression adjustment.
Natural experiments; Leverages exogenous shocks or policies that approximate randomization; Eligibility cutoffs; sudden policy bans; instrumented supply shocks.
Regression discontinuity design; Infers causal effects from a sharp or fuzzy assignment cutoff; Treatment threshold at test score 50; age-based policy eligibility.
Instrumental variables studies; Uses a valid instrument to estimate causal effects under endogeneity; Distance-to-hospital instrument; physician preference instrument.
Propensity score designs; Balances observed confounders in observational comparisons; Matching; weighting (IPTW); subclassification in EHR studies.
Cluster randomized trials; Randomize groups (e.g., clinics, schools) rather than individuals; Practice-level hand hygiene trial; school-based nutrition trial.
Stepped-wedge trials; Sequentially roll out an intervention to clusters on a randomized schedule; Phased EHR alert deployment; staggered vaccination program.
Factorial trials; Test multiple interventions and their interactions efficiently; 2×2 diet×exercise trial; ad copy×layout A/B/n.
Adaptive trials; Prospectively modify features based on interim data; Response-adaptive randomization; sample size re-estimation; dropping arms.
Pragmatic trials; Test effectiveness under real-world conditions with usual care; Broad eligibility RCT in routine clinics; registry-based randomized trial.
N-of-1 trials; Repeated crossovers within one participant to personalize effect estimates; ABAB pain med trial; alternating digital nudges for one user.
SMART trials; Optimize adaptive treatment sequences via multiple randomizations; Sequencing antidepressants with rescue options; stepped intensification.
Micro-randomized trials (JITAI); Rapid, repeated randomization to optimize just-in-time interventions; Mobile prompt timing; context-aware notifications.
A/B testing; Online experiments comparing variants to optimize metrics at scale; Landing page variants; recommender tweaks; email subject lines.
Systematic review; Protocol-driven synthesis addressing a focused question with explicit methods; PRISMA review of RCTs; pre-registered search; risk-of-bias assessment.
Meta-analysis; Quantitatively pools effect sizes across studies (aggregate or IPD); Random-effects pooling; meta-regression; IPD harmonization.
Scoping review; Maps the breadth, concepts, and gaps of a field without formal effect pooling; Landscape of AI in radiology; taxonomy building.
Rapid review; Time-bounded synthesis using streamlined methods for timely decisions; 4-week policy brief; expedited evidence scans.
Umbrella review; Synthesizes findings across multiple systematic reviews; Overview of diet–CVD reviews; higher-level synthesis.
Living review; Continuously updated synthesis as new evidence emerges; Rolling COVID-19 therapy review; auto-updated pipelines.
Realist review; Explains what works, for whom, in what contexts, and why; Mechanism–context–outcome maps; program theories for community health.
Qualitative evidence synthesis (meta-ethnography); Integrates qualitative studies to build higher-order interpretations; Lines-of-argument syntheses; thematic synthesis.
Meta-research; Studies bias, reporting, reproducibility, and methods themselves; P-hacking audits; replication rates; CONSORT adherence.
Bibliometrics / scientometrics; Quantifies publications, citations, and networks to map a field; Co-citation networks; h-index trends; topic modeling.
Psychometrics and instrument development; Designs and validates measures for reliability, validity, and responsiveness; Item generation; factor analysis; Rasch modeling.
Method-comparison studies; Assesses agreement between measurement methods; Bland–Altman analysis; interchangeability of devices; duplicate assays.
Program evaluation; Assesses implementation, outcomes, and impact of real-world programs or policies; Logic models; process/outcome evaluation; realist evaluation.
Implementation science (hybrid Type 1–3); Tests effectiveness while evaluating implementation processes; CFIR-guided rollout; RE-AIM outcomes; facilitation strategies.
Comparative effectiveness research; Compares active alternatives in routine practice; Drug A vs Drug B in EHRs; head-to-head trials; pragmatic registries.
Economic evaluation (CEA/CUA/CBA); Weighs costs against outcomes; Cost per QALY; budget impact analysis; net monetary benefit.
Agent-based modeling; Simulates interacting agents to study emergent system behavior; Epidemic spread model; traffic flow; market dynamics.
System dynamics modeling; Uses stocks, flows, and feedback loops to analyze complex systems over time; Hospital bed occupancy loops; obesity systems map.
Microsimulation / Markov models; Projects individual or state-transition outcomes for policy; Cancer screening states; lifetime cost-effectiveness.
In silico trials; Computational experiments on virtual populations to anticipate real-world effects; Virtual stent testing; simulated dosing regimens.
Predictive modeling / machine learning; Builds and evaluates models to predict outcomes from features; Risk scores; churn prediction; image classification.
Causal discovery / causal ML; Infers causal structure from data under explicit assumptions; PC/FGES; Granger/PCMCI; invariant risk minimization setups.
Network analysis; Studies nodes and ties to reveal structure, centrality, and diffusion; Contact networks; referral patterns; citation networks.
GIS / spatial analysis; Analyzes spatial patterns, clustering, and spatial dependence; Disease hotspot mapping; spatial autocorrelation; kriging.
Delphi method; Iterative expert surveying to build consensus on uncertain topics; Multi-round panels; controlled feedback; consensus thresholds.
Nominal Group Technique; Structured, face-to-face consensus building with prioritized outputs; Idea generation; silent ranking; facilitated voting.
Policy analysis / legal doctrinal research; Interprets statutes and case law to evaluate policy options; Comparative legal analysis; legislative history review.
Scenario planning / futures studies; Explores plausible futures to stress-test strategies; 2×2 scenario matrices; backcasting; horizon scanning.
Design science research; Creates and rigorously evaluates artifacts to solve real problems; Novel algorithm + evaluation; clinical workflow tool; design cycles.
Human-centered design / usability testing; Iteratively designs and tests solutions with end-users; Think-aloud tests; SUS/UMUX-Lite; heuristic evaluation.
Methods paper; Proposes and evaluates a new method, algorithm, or statistical technique; New causal estimator with simulations; comparative performance against baselines.
Protocol paper; Pre-specified plan detailing objectives, design, and analyses before results exist; SPIRIT-compliant RCT protocol; PROSPERO review protocol.
Registered report; Peer review and in-principle acceptance occur before data/results to reduce bias; Stage 1 protocol acceptance; Stage 2 results publication.
Replication study; Repeats a prior study to assess whether findings reproduce under similar conditions; Direct replication of an RCT analysis; lab task replication.
Reproducibility/benchmark study; Independently reruns methods and compares performance on standard datasets; ML re-implementation; challenge leaderboard audit.
Dataset paper / data descriptor; Describes and validates a dataset/resource for reuse with documentation and baselines; Open EEG dataset; multimodal medical image set.
Software paper; Presents a research tool or package with design, implementation, and validation; R package; Python library; web application.
Resource paper; Introduces a reusable biological or computational resource; Cell atlas; reference genome; curated database or biobank.
Negative results paper; Reports well-powered null findings that challenge or contextualize prior claims; No effect of supplement on BP; non-replication of association.
Position paper / perspective; Argues for a viewpoint or framework grounded in evidence; Ethics of LLMs in medicine; risk management perspective.
Technical note / brief communication; Concise report of a small but useful technical advance; Faster preprocessing step; novel surgical maneuver.
Letter / correspondence; Short critique, clarification, or added analysis of a published work; Comment on trial methods; author reply.
Case report; Detailed report of a single clinical patient, typically rare or novel; Drug-induced syndrome; unusual imaging finding.
Case series; Descriptive report of multiple similar clinical cases; 12 patients with a new variant; outcomes after a procedure.
Pilot / feasibility study; Small-scale test of procedures, recruitment, and acceptability to inform a larger study; Pilot RCT; feasibility of home monitoring.
Noninferiority / equivalence trial; Tests whether a new intervention is not worse than or equivalent to standard by a prespecified margin; DOAC vs warfarin; biosimilar equivalence.
Platform trial; Perpetual master protocol evaluating multiple interventions with shared controls; RECOVERY; I-SPY; adaptive arm dropping/adding.
Basket trial; Tests one therapy across multiple diseases or biomarker-defined groups; BRAF inhibitor across cancer types; single-arm cohorts.
Umbrella trial; Tests multiple targeted therapies within one disease stratified by biomarkers; Lung cancer sub-studies; matched therapy arms.
Dose-finding trial (Phase I); Determines safe dosing/scheduling via escalation schemes; 3+3 design; CRM; first-in-human.
Phase II trial; Assesses preliminary efficacy and safety to justify Phase III; Single-arm ORR; Simon’s two-stage design.
Phase III trial; Confirms efficacy and safety in large randomized populations for regulatory approval; Multicenter double-blind superiority RCT.
Phase IV / post-marketing study; Monitors effectiveness and safety in routine use after approval; Registry-based surveillance; rare adverse events detection.
Bioequivalence / bioavailability study; Compares PK metrics to show equivalence or measures absolute/relative F; Cmax/AUC ratios within bounds; generic drug evaluation.
PK/PD study; Characterizes pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics and their relationship; Population PK modeling; exposure–response analysis.
Genome-wide association study (GWAS); Scans the genome for trait associations using large samples and multiple-testing control; GWAS of BMI; meta-GWAS.
Phenome-wide association study (PheWAS); Tests one genetic variant’s association across many phenotypes; Biobank-based phecode screen.
eQTL / QTL mapping; Links genetic variants to expression or trait variation; cis-eQTL in blood; pQTL atlas.
Mendelian randomization; Uses genetic instruments to estimate causal effects of exposures on outcomes; LDL variants as instruments for CAD risk.
Twin / family study; Partitions genetic and environmental contributions via relatedness; ACE models; adoption studies.
Prediction model development; Builds and internally validates diagnostic/prognostic models; LASSO/GBMs; bootstrapping; decision curve analysis.
External validation study; Tests a previously developed prediction model in an independent population; TRIPOD-compliant validation; calibration plots.
Real-world evidence (RWE) study; Uses observational healthcare data to evaluate effectiveness or safety in routine practice; EHR-based comparative effectiveness; claims data analysis.
Registry study; Uses disease/drug registries for longitudinal outcomes; MS registry trajectories; joint replacement registry.
Pharmacovigilance / signal detection; Identifies and evaluates adverse drug events from spontaneous reports or EHRs; Disproportionality analysis; FAERS mining.
Preclinical in vivo study; Tests mechanisms or interventions in animal models; Mouse knockouts; xenograft response.
In vitro / ex vivo study; Experiments on cells, tissues, or organoids outside the organism; CRISPR perturbations; organ-on-a-chip assays.
Clinical audit; Compares practice to explicit standards and implements improvements; Antibiotic timing audit; re-audit loop.
Health Technology Assessment (HTA); Comprehensive evaluation of clinical, economic, and ethical aspects to inform policy; NICE-style appraisal; reimbursement analysis.
Systematic mapping study; Broad, structured catalog of research questions, methods, and gaps; Software engineering landscape; taxonomy map.
Challenge / competition report; Summarizes tasks, datasets, baselines, and results from shared competitions; DREAM challenge; MICCAI challenge overview.
HCI user study; Empirically evaluates usability and user experience of interfaces or systems; Think-aloud sessions; task completion metrics.
Formal verification study; Proves properties of systems or algorithms using logical methods; Model checking; proof assistants.
Algorithm / theory paper; Introduces and analyzes an algorithm with proofs of correctness and complexity; Subquadratic algorithm; approximation ratio bound.
Complexity lower/upper bound paper; Establishes computational hardness or resource bounds; NP-hardness proof; communication complexity bound.
